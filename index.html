المنازل والديار

Abodes and Dwellings

Usāma ibn Munqidh (d. 584/1188)

These are the dwellings of people I once knew
living in ease and matchless honor.

هذي منازل أقوام عهدتهم
في خفض عيش وعز ماله خطر

Arabic text from the Shamela text, based on the edition of Muṣtafā Ḥijāzī, via OpenITI, with a draft machine translation: the verse by GPT-6 Astra, the prose by GPT-6.1 Sol. Scholars are correcting both the Arabic and the English, and their changes appear here as they are made. Start with the uncertain passages.
