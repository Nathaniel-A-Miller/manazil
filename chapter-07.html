On traces (rasm)

فصل في ذكر الرسم

بشر بن أبي خازم، من الوافر

Bishr ibn Abī Khāzim said:

قال بشر بن أبي خازم:

The traces at Rāmah have faded, and in the ravines,and on the dunes of al-Ḥuḍayr as far as Lifāʿ.
عفا رسم برامة فالتلاعفكثبان الحضير إلى لفاع
Every cloud pouring torrents has effaced them,its sound resembling the sound of reed pipes.
عفاها كل هطال سكوبيشبه صوته صوت اليراع

(Al-yarāʿ: reeds used as pipes for whistling. Another reading is “the sound of camel calves” (ṣawt al-rubāʿ). One says rubaʿ and rubāʿ of a she-camel’s young when it is ten days old.)

(اليراع: القصب التي يصفر بها، ويروى صوت الرباع يقال: ربع ورباع وهو ولد الناقة إذا كان له عشرة أيام) .

I stood there questioning them at length,but there was none there to answer a caller.
وقفت بها أسائلها طويلاوما فيها مجاوبة لداع
Their people packed up and departed from them,and the dwellings made me weep in dismay.
تحمل أهلها منها فبانوافأبكتني المنازل للرواع
Translator's notes (2)
  • Line 1: The places الحضير and لفاع are not securely identified; the vocalizations al-Ḥuḍayr and Lifāʿ are tentative. التلاع is taken as the common noun 'ravines,' though it could be a place name.
  • Line 4: للرواع is obscure here and may be corrupt. 'In dismay' tentatively connects it with روع, 'fright'; the intended sense is uncertain.

مزاحم العقيلي، من الطويل

And Muzāḥim ibn al-Ḥārith al-ʿUqaylī said:

وقال مزاحم بن الحارث العقيلي:

Did the traces at al-Naqʿ stir your longing this morning—effaced, their last days of habitation long ago?
أشاقتك بالنقع الغداة رسومدوارس أدنى عهدهن قديم
Effaced: their people have packed upand gone away, while their tent frames remain.
دوارس أما أهلها فتحملوافبانوا، وأما خيمها فمقيم
These are but traces of a company once united,scattered by their destinations after living side by side.
وما ذاك إلا من جميع تفرقتبهم نية بعد الجوار قسم
They became like a striped cloak torn apart:one packing up and leaving at dawn, another staying behind.
فعادوا كبرد العصب شق فأصبحوافمحتمل غاد، وظل مقيم
Such is separation’s way—it never fails mewhenever I have a neighbor dear to me.
وذلك دأب للنوى ليس مخلفيإذا كان لي جار علي كريم
Translator's notes (4)
  • Line 1: Al-Naqʿ is taken as a place-name; its location is uncertain. أدنى عهدهن may refer to the most recent habitation of the sites or the speaker’s last acquaintance with them.
  • Line 2: خيم can denote tents or their wooden frameworks; the latter sense is used here.
  • Line 3: The final word قسم is unclear and may be corrupted; as printed, it also appears inconsistent with the other rhyme endings. The translation follows the apparent overall sense, taking نية as the destination or direction of departure.
  • Line 4: وظل مقيم is read as 'and another remained.' Without vocalization, ظل could instead be the noun 'shade,' yielding 'a lingering shade.'

أبو تمام، من البسيط

And Abū Tammām said:

وقال أبو تمام:

As for the traces, they have recalled what is past;so do not let your two tear ducts cease to flow.
أما الرسوم فقد أذكرن ما سلفافلا تكفن عن شأنيك أن يقفا
The lovesick man has no excuse for finding solace, norhave tears any excuse for stopping after the tribe has gone,
لا عذر للصب أن يقنى السلو، ولاللدمع بعد مضي الحي أن يقفا
until he remains amid streaming tears and blood,at the campsite, seemingly bleeding from his eyes.
حتى يظل بماء سافح ودمفي الربع يحسب من عينيه قد رعفا
Translator's notes (1)
  • Line 1: شأنيك is understood as 'your two tear ducts.' The construction فلا تكفن عن شأنيك أن يقفا is unclear and may be corrupt; the translation follows the apparent injunction to continue weeping.

ابن الخياط، من الطويل

And Abū ʿAbd Allah ibn al-Khayyāṭ al-Dimashqī said:

وقال أبو عبد الله بن الخياط الدمشقي:

Here are the traces—if standing over traces could avail;that is prudence—were you not so long estranged from prudence.
هو الرسم لو أغنى الوقوف على الرسمهو الحزم لولا بعد عهدك بالحزم
That evening, the heart went utterly mad,and my longing contended with me like an adversary.
عشية جن القلب فيها جنونهونازعني شوقي منازعة الخصم
When it would accept nothing but weeping in sorrow,I wept until I left not a trace of those remains.
فلما أبى إلا البكاء على الأسىبكيت فما أبقيت للرسم من رسم
The dwellings felt my longing for their people;had they not felt my longing, they would not have sickened as I did.
لقد وجدت وجدي الديار بأهلهاولو لم تجد وجدي لما سقمت سقمي
Effaced dwellings, whose wasting grieved me—why did one wasted in heart and body not grieve them?
منازل أدراس شجاني نحولهافهلا شجاها ناحل القلب والجسم؟
Rain watered them before me; then, when I watered themwith my tears, they saw how the later rain surpassed the first autumn showers.
سقاها الحيا قبلي فلما سقيتهادموي رأت فضل الولي على الوسمي
Translator's notes (3)
  • Line 1: The referent of هو in هو الحزم ('that is prudence') is unclear; it may refer to standing at the traces.
  • Line 3: The subject of أبى ('would accept nothing') could be either the heart or the speaker's longing.
  • Line 6: دموي appears to be a misprint for دموعي ('my tears'), which is the reading translated here.

زهير بن أبي سلمى، من الكامل

And Zuhayr ibn Abī Sulmā al-Muzanī said:

وقال زهير بن أبي سلمى المزني:

The campsite’s familiar traces stirred my heart,desolate at Dhū al-Haḍabāt, like a tattoo.
هاج الفؤاد معارف الرسمقفرا بذي الهضبات كالوشم
I used to see it with its people dwelling there,band after band—what fine bands they were!
ولقد أراها والحلول بهامن بعد صرم أيما صرم
But this morning Time claimed them for itself;Time shoots at me, and I do not shoot back.
فاستأثر الدهر الغداة بهموالدهر يرميني ولا أرمي
Were it an adversary I could match in archery,my arrow would not stray when my anger rose.
لو كان لي قرنا أناضلهما طاش عند حفيظة سهمي
Or if it would deal fairly, I would tell it:“You have secured your share; leave mine alone.”
أو كان يعطي النصف قلت لهأحرزت قسمك فاله عن قسمي
Translator's notes (2)
  • Line 1: Dhū al-Haḍabāt appears to be a place-name; its location is uncertain.
  • Line 2: The syntax of “من بعد صرم أيما صرم” is uncertain. The translation takes ṣirm as a band of people and understands the phrase as praising successive groups of inhabitants.

الصمة القشيري، من الطويل

And al-Ṣimma al-Qushayrī said:

وقال الصمة القشيري:

My two friends, turn aside today—or leave it be—let us greet the desolate traces at al-Qubayba.
خليلي عوجا منكما اليوم أو دعانحيي رسوما بالقبيبة بلقعا
The winds persisted there until they swept awayits recognizable features, save the flat stones laid in place,
أربت بها الأرواح حتى تنسفتمعارفها إلا الصفيح الموضعا
And three stones in the dwellings, as though they werethree doves perched facing one another.
وغير ثلاث في الديار كأنهاثلاث حمامات تقابلن وقعا
My left eye wept; when I rebuked it for folly after forbearance, both eyes streamed together.
بكيت عيني اليسرى فلما زجرتها عن الجهل بعد الحلم أسبلتا معا
Translator's notes (3)
  • Line 1: The object of أو دعا, literally 'or leave [it],' is unstated; the precise force of this alternative is uncertain.
  • Line 3: The noun following 'three' is omitted; hearthstones are presumably intended.
  • Line 4: بكيت appears to be a misprint for بكت, 'wept,' which is the reading translated. As written, it could alternatively be vocalized بكّيت, 'I made [my eye] weep.'

البحترى، من الخفيف

May lavender rise in bloom upon your sandy knoll, and may the clouds pour rain upon you. Al-Buḥturī said:

تعالى فوق أجرعك الخزامي ... بنور، واستهل بك الغمام وقال البحتري:

What harm to the riders if their mounts haltin the haunts of youth and the traces of youthful love?
ما على الركب من وقوف الركابفي مغاني الصبى ورسم التصابي
Where are the people of the tents at al-Ajzaʿ al-Fard?They have departed—oh, where are the people of the tents!
أين أهل القباب بالأجزع الفرد، تولوا، لا أين أهل القباب!
Turn aside: if I weep among the traces, the tearsare mine, and the sorrow is mine.
عرجوا فالدموع إن أبك في الرسم، دموعي والاكتئاب اكتئابي
Like my loved ones—if the reproacher only knew—are the dwellings of my loved ones to me.
وكمثل الأحباب لو يعلم العاذل عندي منازل الأحباب
Translator's notes (2)
  • Line 2: Al-Ajzaʿ al-Fard is unidentified; it may be a descriptive expression rather than a place name. The spelling الأجزع may also be a misprint for الأجرع, a sandy tract. The word al-Fard is divided between the source hemistichs.
  • Line 2: The wording لا أين is syntactically unusual and possibly corrupt. It is provisionally rendered as an emphatic repetition of the question: 'oh, where ...!'

ذو الرمة، من الطويل

And Dhū al-Rumma said:

وقال ذو الرمة:

Have the worn-away traces of the encampments stirred youby the high, jutting sandhills of Ḥawḍā?
أهاجتك أطلال الرسوم الدوائربأدعاص حوضي المغنقات النوادر

نعم هاجت الأطلال شوقا كفى بهمن الشوق إلا أنه غير ظاهر
I kept concealing my feelings, until it was as thoughat Dhū al-Ramth they had never crossed anyone’s mind,
فما زلت أطوى النفس حتى كأنهابذي الرمث لم تخطر على بال خاطر
out of shame and fear that my fellow riders might seesome sign of the secrets stored within the heart.

Translator's notes (2)
  • Line 1: المغنقات is unclear and may be a misprint for المعنقات, describing something rising high or projecting; 'high' follows that conjecture. النوادر is taken to describe projecting or isolated dunes. حوضي is read as the place-name Ḥawḍā (حوضى).
  • Line 3: The feminine pronoun in كأنها has an uncertain referent. The translation takes it to refer to النفس, understood here as the speaker’s inward feelings; a reference to an unmentioned beloved is also possible. Dhū al-Ramth is treated as a place-name, but its location is unidentified.

Translator's notes (3)
  • Line 2: طعن, divided between the hemistichs, is taken in the sense of journeying. It may instead be a misprint for ظعن, “departed.”

  • Line 7: نجن appears corrupt. The translation tentatively reads يجن, “conceals” or “harbors,” with “heart” as its subject.

وقلت:

I stood by the traces of a dwelling in a barren wilderness,empty of company, silent when called upon.
وقفت على رسم ببيداء بلقعخلي من النادي صموت إذا دعي
